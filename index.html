To my moon

Meri jaan,

I wanted the first thing you saw today to be something from me, even from the other side of the world.

Before you, I never paid much attention to the moon. Now I look for it every night. Some nights it’s full and bright, some nights it’s only a thin silver line, and some nights I can’t see it at all. But I know it’s always there. That’s how I love you: on the loud days and the quiet ones, when you’re happy, when you’re quiet, when you’re silly and when you have no words left. I love every version of you.

I hope you know how strong you are. You carry so much with so much grace, you never give up, and you still find a way to be kind to everyone around you. I admire you more than I can ever say, and I am so proud of the woman you are. I’m not going anywhere, Insha Allah. And if I have ever hurt you, I am truly sorry. Please always tell me, because your heart matters to me more than anything. I will always listen, and I will always choose you.

Your voice late at night is the softest part of my day. I keep wishing I could be there in person, to bring you flowers, to hold your hand, to watch the moon rise with you instead of on a screen. Every day that passes brings December a little closer, and I count every one of them.

Every night I make dua for us. I ask Allah to bless what we are building and to make our love a source of peace and joy. I ask Him to give us patience through the distance and the waiting, and strength whenever we are tested, so that every test brings us even closer. And I ask Him to write us a future together: one home, flowers on the table, and the moon outside our window. Rabbana afrigh ‘alayna sabran. Our Lord, pour patience upon us. Ameen.

Until then, look up tonight. The moon you love is the same one I’m looking at. Wherever you are, we’re under the same sky, and my heart is right there with you.

And for today: I hope your Monday is gentle with you. May everything you do feel a little lighter, may something make you laugh out loud, and may you smile at least once and think of me. Go have a beautiful day, meri jaan. I’ll be right here when it’s done.

Forever yours,

Majou
